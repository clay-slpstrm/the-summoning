# Art Prompts — The Summoning Launch Assets

Copy-pasteable image generation prompts for the 20 artifact NFTs plus bonus
launch assets. Designed to be tool-agnostic (Midjourney, DALL-E 3 / ChatGPT,
Nanobanana, Stable Diffusion variants). Per-tool syntax notes at the bottom.

The goal is a visually cohesive collection: 20 artifacts that feel like
fragments pulled from the same dark mythology. Generate one image you love
first, then use that as a style reference (`--sref` in MJ, "in the same style
as the attached image" in DALL-E) for the rest.

---

## 1. Style Sheet — paste at the start of every prompt

```
A dark Lovecraftian occult artifact, fragmented relic recovered from a
forbidden ritual, eldritch fantasy oil painting style with subtle digital
glitch. Deep velvet purple #4c1d95 and void black #0a0a0f base palette, with
selective neon accents in ritual purple #7c3aed, blood red #EF4444, and pale
candlelight gold. Composition: square centered, single hero object with
shadow-shrouded background, soft volumetric haze, hint of ancient stone or
parchment substrate behind. No text, no watermarks, no human figures unless
specifically called for. Mood: foreboding, sacred, ancient.
```

Length tip: most tools handle ~80–120 words well. The style sheet above is
~60 — leaves room for the per-artifact details below.

## 2. Old One imagery library

Use these visual cues alongside the style sheet. Each Old One has a distinct
iconography drawn from H.P. Lovecraft's mythos so the 4 artifacts within a set
feel like they belong together.

### 1. Cthulhu — "the Dreaming One"
Visual cues: massive tentacled cephalopod-dragon, drowned cyclopean architecture,
green-tinged scales beneath dark water, an unblinking saucer-shaped eye, drowned
city silhouette in background, kelp-strung ritual implements, deep ocean
phosphorescence.

### 2. Nyarlathotep — "the Crawling Chaos"
Visual cues: a thousand-faced messenger, shifting flesh, obsidian black mask
with golden hieroglyphs, swarms of small dark fluttering shapes, Egyptian
pharaonic motifs corrupted, beckoning gloved hand, mirrors that don't reflect
correctly, telephone-line shadows against a blood-red sky.

### 3. Azathoth — "the Blind Idiot God"
Visual cues: a churning nuclear sun at the center of all things, blind cosmic
mouth, broken flute fragments, swirling galactic dust, geometric impossibilities,
prismatic light fractures, eyeless attendants in piping robes, kaleidoscopic
fractal madness.

### 4. Shub-Niggurath — "the Black Goat of the Woods"
Visual cues: an ancient horned goat-deity with a thousand young, twisted forest
of black branches, fertility runes carved into bark, milk-white moon, hoofprints
in pale ash, antlers wreathed in offerings, woodland depths swallowing light,
small embryonic shapes nestled among roots.

### 5. Yog-Sothoth — "the Gate and the Key"
Visual cues: a teeming cluster of iridescent spheres of varying size, gateway
between dimensions, geometric portal rings overlapping, fractal keys floating
mid-rotation, refracted starlight, sigils that move when you don't look at them,
spaces between spaces, the doorway and the doorkeeper at once.

## 3. Tier compositional framing

These cue the composition/mood for the tier, regardless of Old One.

| Tier | Mood | Composition cue |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Shattered Ritual** (failed epoch) | Mournful, dim, fragmentary | Broken ritual artifact lying half-buried in ash, dim grey-violet light, sense of failure and dissipation. The Old One's presence withdrawing. |
| **1 — Harbinger** (top ~1%) | Triumphant, radiant, dominating | Full ceremonial regalia of the Old One, central blazing presence, dramatic backlight, gold + tier-color accents at maximum saturation. The Old One acknowledging the bearer. |
| **2 — Acolyte** (top ~10%) | Devoted, ascending, focused | A devotional object — sacred vestment, prayer-shard, or chalice — in mid-ritual use, candle-warm under-lighting, robed silhouette suggested behind. Mid-saturation tier color. |
| **3 — Cultist** (everyone else) | Faithful, weathered, participant | A simple worn relic of devotion — beads, ring, signet, mask fragment — held aloft against ritual smoke, lower saturation, more shadow than light. The cult mark visible. |

## 6. Tool-specific tips

### Midjourney (v6 / v7)
- Append `--ar 1:1 --v 7 --style raw --stylize 250` to artifact prompts.
- For the wide banners: `--ar 21:9 --style raw`.
- **Lock in cohesion**: generate one artifact you love, grab its `--sref <number>`,
  then add that flag to every subsequent gen. Or use `--cref <url>` to
  reference uploaded images for consistency.
- Avoid `--niji` (anime style) for this collection — pure realistic / illustrative.

### DALL-E 3 / ChatGPT
- Just paste the full prompt (style sheet + variant). DALL-E auto-handles
  aspect ratio if you say "square" / "wide cinematic" in the prompt.
- For style consistency, attach the first generated image to subsequent chats
  and say "match the style, color palette, and lighting of the attached image."
- DALL-E sometimes adds extraneous text — append: *"No text, letters, words,
  or watermarks anywhere in the image."*

### Nanobanana (Gemini Imagen)
- Closer to DALL-E in syntax — natural language. Same prompts work as-is.
- Strong with subtle lighting and color grading; good fit for the candle-warm /
  dim ritual atmosphere.
- For consistency: drop the first-generated image into the chat and reference
  it as the style anchor.

### Stable Diffusion (SDXL / Flux)
- Prepend positive: `masterpiece, highly detailed, dark Lovecraftian oil
  painting, dramatic lighting, 8k`
- Add a negative prompt: `text, watermark, signature, low quality, blurry,
  cartoonish, anime, modern, contemporary clothing, color photography`
- Steps: 30+. CFG: 6–8. Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras or DPM++ SDE.
- Use the same seed across an Old One's 4 tiers for tighter visual consistency
  within a set.

## 7. Production workflow recommendation

1. **Pick one Old One** (e.g., Cthulhu — set 1) and generate the **Harbinger
   (1/1)** first. That's the most visually rich prompt and will set the tone.
2. Iterate until you have a Harbinger you love. **Save the seed / style
   reference**.
3. Generate the other 3 tiers (0, 2, 3) within that set using the same seed /
   style reference. Adjust per-tier composition cues from §3 as needed.
4. Repeat for the remaining 4 Old Ones (8 hours of iteration is realistic for
   AI-gen; 20–40 hours if you're hand-illustrating).
5. **Export at 1024×1024 PNG**, drop into `backend/public/artifacts/{1..5}/{0..3}.png`.
6. The backend serves them automatically — no restart needed (Express picks up
   file changes on the static handler).
7. (Optional but recommended) Generate the 5 bonus assets in §5 — they're what
   collectors and lurkers see on OpenSea / social before they ever connect a
   wallet.
